setup-install --barebones [--select codex_cli,claude_code,agent_instances,codex_hook_trust] [--codex-instances COUNT] [--claude-instances COUNT] [--home PATH]
--list-components lists the barebones components; --help prints this help.
Without --select, installs Codex and Claude. Counts are optional and include account 1.
--codex-source {fork|npm} selects the local fork (default) or official @openai/codex@latest package.
CODEX_INSTALL_SOURCE supplies the source when the flag is absent; the flag overrides it.
CODEX_NPM_PREFIX overrides the dedicated user-local Codex installation directory.
--skip-hook-trust-confirmation saves the hook-trust bypass (default); --require-hook-trust-confirmation restores review.
The saved choice applies to the common Codex runtime and managed account aliases.
